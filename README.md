# CareerOS
An agentic career intelligence platform for job discovery, skill development, and interview preparation.

Begin date:11/09/2026
## Motivation 
The biggest challenge for most job seekers is not writing a resume, but understanding which role truely fit them and what they need to improve to become a strong candidate.

This platform will be built to bridge the gap between where you are today and your dream role through continously feedback, target improvement and consistent action.

Let's make your dream come true.
